Self-Drive Terms & Conditions

Package & Limits

  • For 24 hrs a 350 km limit is given. If km exceeds 350, ₹5 per km for a 5 seater and ₹7 per km for a 7 seater will be charged.
  • If time exceeds 24 hrs, ₹150 per hour will be charged.

Charges & Deposits

  • Deposit: Customer's bike / ₹5,000*.
  • Booking advance is non-refundable.
  • Extra fuel is non-refundable.
  • If your time exceeds 7 hrs, a full-day charge will be applied.

Care & Responsibility

  • Dents, long scratches, breakage, etc. cannot be claimed in insurance.
  • Don't drink and drive — if you do, you are fully responsible for any damage.
  • Maintain the initial level of fuel, otherwise charges may apply.

Documents at Pick-up

  • Please carry ORIGINAL Aadhaar, PAN and driving licence at the time of pick-up.
